      Behind the scenes

      The life of a submission.

      1. The writer sends a pitch.
      2. It waits in the publisher’s queue as “In review”.
      3. The publisher accepts, asks for changes or declines.
      4. If changes are requested, the writer can resubmit.

      Good to know: this demo stores data in your browser (localStorage), so there is no real account or server.
